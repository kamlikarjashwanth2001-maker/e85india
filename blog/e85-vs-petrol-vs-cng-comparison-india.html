❓ Common Questions

Q: E85 vs CNG - konsa zyada paisa bachata hai?

CNG slightly cheaper hai (₹3.06/km vs E85 ₹4.37/km). Lekin CNG kit cost ₹50,000+ hai, jabki E85 ka koi additional cost nahi. Long-term me E85 better agar new vehicle khareed rahe hai.

Q: Konsi fuel environment ke liye best hai?

E85 sabse green hai! 85% kam pollution vs petrol. Renewable bhi (sugarcane se). CNG 30% less pollution karta hai. Petrol sabse zyada pollute karti hai.

Q: Kya petrol car me CNG kit lagana acha hai?

CNG kit lagana commercial vehicles ke liye good (high usage). Personal cars ke liye 2-3 saal me cost recover hota hai. Per power 15% kam ho jaati hai, boot space kam hota hai.

Q: E85 cars me convert kar sakte hai?

Haan, flex fuel conversion kit (₹15,000-25,000) lagayi ja sakti hai modern petrol cars (2015+) me. Lekin factory FFV vehicles best hai warranty aur performance ke liye.

Q: Petrol abhi tak best kyun hai?

Petrol abhi everywhere available hai (80,000+ stations). No setup needed. Trips me convenient. Lekin future me E85/EV replace karenge petrol ko slowly.

Q: CNG safe hai ya dangerous?

CNG technically safe hai but accident me cylinder risk hota hai. Petrol & E85 liquid form me safer. CNG vehicles regular inspection important hai.
