🚗 Top 5 Upcoming Flex Fuel Cars in India (E85 Ready)

Get ready for the green revolution with these highly anticipated E85 cars!

India is rapidly moving towards a greener and more cost-effective future. With the introduction of E85 fuel across major cities, automobile giants are gearing up to launch their flex-fuel vehicles (FFVs). If you're planning to buy a car in the near future and want to save heavily on fuel costs (E85 is almost 21% cheaper than petrol!), you should wait for these upcoming flex-fuel cars.

Here are the Top 5 highly anticipated E85-ready Flex Fuel Cars expected to hit Indian roads very soon!

1. Maruti Suzuki WagonR Flex Fuel

🔥 The People's Car Goes Green!

Expected Launch: Late 2026

Expected Price: ₹6.5 Lakh - ₹8.0 Lakh

Maruti Suzuki showcased the WagonR Flex Fuel prototype at the Auto Expo, and it garnered massive attention. Designed specifically for India, it can run on anywhere between 20% (E20) to 85% (E85) ethanol blends. This will make the WagonR one of the most budget-friendly cars to run on E85, ensuring running costs are incredibly low.

2. Toyota Corolla Altis Flex Fuel

🌟 The Premium Hybrid FFV

Expected Launch: Mid 2027

Expected Price: ₹20.0 Lakh - ₹25.0 Lakh

Toyota, a pioneer in flex-fuel technology in Brazil, brought the Corolla Altis Flex Fuel to India as a pilot project. Combining strong hybrid technology with flex-fuel capability, this car promises unprecedented mileage and ultra-low emissions. It is expected to be a game-changer for the premium sedan segment.

3. Tata Nexon Flex Fuel Edition

🛡️ India's Favorite SUV gets E85 Ready

Expected Launch: Early 2027

Expected Price: ₹9.0 Lakh - ₹14.0 Lakh

Tata Motors is aggressive with its green mobility push. While their EVs are dominating, they are also working on flex-fuel engines for their popular models like the Nexon. Expected to feature a revamped 1.2L turbocharged engine tuned for E85, it will offer the perfect blend of performance, safety, and cheap running costs.

4. Hyundai Creta FFV

✨ The SUV King Adapts

Expected Launch: Late 2027

Expected Price: ₹12.0 Lakh - ₹19.0 Lakh

Hyundai isn't sitting quietly. With Brazil already enjoying Hyundai's flex-fuel tech, the company is preparing to localize this technology for the Indian Creta. An E85 compatible Creta would make SUV ownership much cheaper, appealing to long-distance drivers and daily commuters alike.

5. Mahindra XUV300 Flex

🚀 Thrilling Performance on Ethanol

Expected Launch: Mid 2027

Expected Price: ₹9.5 Lakh - ₹15.0 Lakh

Known for powerful engines, Mahindra is tweaking its mStallion turbo-petrol engines to support E85 fuel. The XUV300 (or its successor, XUV 3XO) is likely to be the first recipient, delivering punchy torque without the massive petrol bills!

💡 Why Wait for a Flex Fuel Car?

  • Massive Savings: E85 is significantly cheaper than standard petrol, saving you thousands every year.
  • Better Performance: E85 has a higher octane rating, meaning better engine performance and horsepower.
  • Eco-Friendly: Drastically reduces greenhouse gas emissions, keeping our cities cleaner.
  • Future-Proof: As the government phases in higher ethanol blends, an E85 car ensures you're ready for the future.
