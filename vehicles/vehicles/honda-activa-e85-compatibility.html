⚠️ Quick Answer: Is Honda Activa E85 Compatible?

Honda Activa Flex Fuel (2024+) is fully E85 compatible. Older Honda Activa models (1999-2023) are NOT E85 compatible and need a Flex Fuel Conversion Kit (₹15,000-20,000) to safely use E85 fuel.

Honda launched the Activa Flex Fuel variant in 2024 as part of India's National Biofuels Policy. This new model is one of India's first flex fuel scooters, designed to run on E85 (85% ethanol + 15% petrol), making it eco-friendly and economical.

🔧 How to Convert Old Honda Activa to Use E85

If you own an older Honda Activa (1999-2023) and want to use E85:

  1. Buy a Flex Fuel Conversion Kit (₹15,000-20,000)
  2. Get it installed by certified mechanic
  3. Replace fuel lines with ethanol-resistant ones
  4. Update ECU mapping for E85
  5. Test ride and tune as needed

⚠️ Warning: Conversion may void your warranty. Consult authorized service center before installing.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use E85 in my old Honda Activa?

No, older Honda Activa models (before 2024) are NOT designed for E85. Using E85 can damage fuel lines, gaskets, and engine. You'll need a Flex Fuel Conversion Kit (₹15,000-20,000) or upgrade to Honda Activa Flex Fuel 2024+.

What is the price of Honda Activa Flex Fuel in India?

Honda Activa Flex Fuel is priced at approximately ₹78,000 (ex-showroom) in India. Price may vary by 5-10% based on city, state taxes, and dealership location. On-road price typically ranges ₹85,000-92,000.

How much mileage does Honda Activa give on E85?

Honda Activa Flex Fuel gives approximately 60 km/L on E85, compared to 50 km/L of regular Activa on petrol. While slightly less efficient per liter, E85 is 21% cheaper, resulting in net savings of ₹6,500+ per year.

Is Honda Activa Flex Fuel available in all cities?

Honda Activa Flex Fuel is being rolled out across major Indian cities. Currently available in Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Pune, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, and Ahmedabad. Check with your nearest Honda dealer for availability.

Will using E85 void Honda Activa's warranty?

No, using E85 in Honda Activa Flex Fuel (2024+) will NOT void warranty as it's designed for E85. However, using E85 in non-flex fuel Activa models WILL void warranty and may cause engine damage.

Where can I buy Honda Activa Flex Fuel?

Visit your nearest authorized Honda 2-wheeler dealership. You can also book online through Honda's official website or platforms like BikeWale, BikeDekho. Test rides are available at most dealerships.

What is the difference between Activa 6G and Activa Flex Fuel?

The main difference is the FUEL TYPE. Activa 6G runs on regular petrol (E10/E20), while Activa Flex Fuel runs on E85 (85% ethanol). Activa Flex Fuel has modified engine components, fuel lines, and ECU mapping to handle ethanol's properties.
