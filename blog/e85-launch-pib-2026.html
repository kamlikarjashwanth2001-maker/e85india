Government Launches E85 Fuel in India

June 5, 2026 - Union Minister Hardeep Singh Puri launched E85 fuel at an IndianOil outlet in New Delhi.

Key Highlights

  • 48 E85 stations across India now
  • 500 stations by December 2026
  • 5,000 stations by December 2027
  • E85 is ₹20/litre cheaper than petrol

Environmental Benefits

  • 61% lower greenhouse gas emissions
  • Near-zero particulate matter

⚠️ Important

E85 is ONLY for Flex Fuel Vehicles (FFVs). Regular cars cannot use E85.
