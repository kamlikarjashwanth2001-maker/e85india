🤔 E85 Itna Sasta Kyun Hai?

E85 fuel petrol se 21% cheaper hai kyunki:

  • 🌾 India me banta hai - Sugarcane aur agricultural waste se
  • 🚫 Import nahi karna padta - Foreign exchange save
  • 💰 Lower taxes - Government promote kar rahi hai
  • 🇮🇳 Local production - Indian kisaano ko fayda
  • ⛽ Less processing cost - Direct ethanol

❓ E85 Price Related FAQs

Q: E85 fuel ka aaj ka price kya hai?

Aaj E85 fuel ka India average price ₹74.28/L hai. Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru me slightly different prices. Daily updated rates upar table me hai.

Q: E85 petrol se kitna cheaper hai?

E85 petrol se 21.6% cheaper hai! Petrol ₹94.72/L hai, E85 sirf ₹74.28/L. Matlab ₹20.44 per liter saving!

Q: Sabse cheap E85 kaunsi city me hai?

Lucknow (₹73.78/L) aur Pune (₹73.85/L) me sabse cheap E85 mil rahi hai. Hyderabad me bhi best saving (₹33.36/L vs petrol).

Q: E85 fuel price daily change hoti hai?

Haan, E85 prices daily update hoti hai just like petrol. Government oil companies (IOCL, BPCL, HPCL) daily rates set karti hai based on production cost.

Q: 10,000 km/year drive karne pe kitna bachega?

Agar aap 10,000 km/year drive karte hai E85 par, toh ₹12,024 saalana bachega compared to petrol. 5 saal me ₹60,000+ saving!

Q: E85 price kab aur kam hogi?

Experts ke according, 2026 tak E85 price ₹65-72/L tak gir sakti hai jab government 2000+ stations open karegi. Mass production se prices aur sasti hongi.

Q: E85 vs CNG me konsa cheaper?

Cost per KM me CNG slightly cheaper (₹3.06/km vs E85 ₹3.71/km). Lekin E85 ki availability badh rahi hai, CNG kit cost bhi zyada hai. Long term me E85 better!
