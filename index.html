The Science

What is E85 Fuel?

E85 is a high-performance blend of 85% ethanol and 15% petrol — cleaner, cheaper, and purpose-built for India's green energy transition.

01
Sugarcane-Derived Ethanol
India produces ethanol primarily from sugarcane molasses and damaged grains. The fermentation and distillation process creates high-purity fuel-grade ethanol, reducing dependence on crude oil imports.
Renewable Domestic Carbon Neutral
02
Flex-Fuel Vehicle Technology
FFVs are equipped with modified fuel systems, sensors, and ECUs that automatically detect the ethanol blend ratio and adjust engine parameters for optimal performance and efficiency.
Smart ECU Auto Detect Multi-Fuel
03
Clean Combustion Profile
E85 burns cleaner than petrol — with up to 32% lower lifecycle CO₂ emissions, significantly reduced particulate matter, and lower hydrocarbon pollutants contributing to better urban air quality.

FAQ
Everything You Need to Know
Common questions about E85 fuel, flex-fuel vehicles, and India's ethanol blending programme.
Ask a Question →
What exactly is E85 fuel? +
E85 is a high-ethanol fuel blend containing 85% denatured ethanol and 15% petrol. It has an exceptionally high octane rating (RON ~108), significantly lower CO₂ lifecycle emissions, and is produced domestically from sugarcane, grains, or agricultural waste — making it a truly renewable and indigenous energy source for India.
Can any car use E85 in India? +
No — only Flex-Fuel Vehicles (FFVs) specifically certified to run on E85 can safely use this blend. Standard petrol vehicles are designed for E5–E10 at most; using E85 in a non-FFV can damage fuel system components including seals, pumps, and injectors. Use our Vehicle Compatibility Checker above to verify your car.
Is E85 cheaper than petrol in India? +
E85 is typically priced 30–40% lower per litre than petrol. However, ethanol has a lower energy density (~34% less than petrol), so FFVs experience 15–25% lower fuel efficiency. The net running cost savings are around 15–20% per kilometre, making E85 economically attractive for high-mileage users.
Where can I find E85 fuel stations in India? +
E85 stations are currently operational across select IOCL, BPCL, and HPCL outlets, primarily in Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, and Karnataka. The network is expanding rapidly under the National Biofuel Policy. Use our live station map and search feature to locate the nearest E85 dispenser to you.
Does E85 affect engine performance? +
In a properly tuned FFV, E85 can actually improve performance due to its higher octane rating and better heat of vaporisation, which cools the intake charge and allows for more aggressive ignition timing. Manufacturers like Toyota have demonstrated 5–8% peak power increases in E85-optimised engines compared to petrol.
What is India's ethanol blending policy timeline? +
India's National Biofuel Policy targets 20% ethanol blending in petrol (E20) nationally by 2025, with the long-term vision of supporting higher blends including E85 for FFV adoption. The government has set procurement prices for ethanol, mandated FFV production by automakers, and established a robust supply chain through the Ethanol Blended Petrol (EBP) Programme.
