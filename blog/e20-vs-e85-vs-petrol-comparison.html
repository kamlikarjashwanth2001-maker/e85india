🌍 Environmental Impact

  • ✅ E85: 61% lower lifecycle emissions than petrol
  • ✅ E20: 15-20% lower emissions
  • ✅ E85: Near-zero particulate matter → cleaner city air

⚡ Performance: Octane Rating

Higher octane = better engine performance, less knocking, smoother ride.

  • Petrol: 91 RON (Standard)
  • E20: ~95 RON (Good)
  • E85: ~108 RON (Best for performance)

🏁 Verdict: Which Fuel Should You Choose?

✅ If you have a Flex Fuel Vehicle (FFV): Choose E85. It's cheaper, cleaner, and gives better performance.

✅ If you have a normal petrol car (2020+): Choose E20. It's government-approved and slightly cheaper than petrol.

✅ If you have an older petrol car (pre-2019): Stick to regular Petrol or consult your manufacturer about E20 compatibility.
