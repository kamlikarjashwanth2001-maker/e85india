🚗 Complete List of Flex Fuel Vehicles in India (2026)

E85 compatible cars, bikes, and upcoming models — updated June 2026

✅ What is a Flex Fuel Vehicle (FFV)? FFVs can run on any ethanol blend from E20 to E100. They have special fuel injectors, ethanol sensors, and corrosion-resistant components.

🔍 How to identify an FFV: Look for a YELLOW fuel filler cap or "Flex Fuel" badge on the vehicle.
